Privacy Policy

Amazon FBA & FBM Calculator: Profit, ROI & Monthly Sales · Effective November 17, 2026 · Last updated November 17, 2026

The short version: the extension reads product details from the amazon.com page you're viewing, calculates fees and profit inside your browser, and saves the costs you type on your own device. It never sends any data to EcomsTool or to anyone else. We don't sell, share, or analyze your data, because we never receive it.

Who we are

EcomsTool is an independent developer of Chrome extensions for online sellers. This policy covers the Chrome extension "Amazon FBA & FBM Calculator: Profit, ROI & Monthly Sales" (the "extension"). Contact: ecomstool@gmail.com.

Information the extension reads

The extension's code is loaded only on amazon.com product detail pages. It does not read anything on the page until you open the calculator panel, using the toolbar icon or keyboard shortcut. If you turn on "Stay open while browsing," the panel reopens on the next product page in the same tab, and reads that page too.

When the panel is open, the extension reads these publicly displayed product details from the current page:

  • ASIN, product title, and price
  • Package or product dimensions and weight
  • Category and Best Sellers Rank
  • Amazon's "bought in past month" label, if shown
  • Number of offers, who the item is sold by, and whether the listing has variations

This information is used only to calculate fees, profit, ROI and estimated sales in the panel. It is processed in your browser and is never transmitted off your device.

Information stored on your device

The extension uses Chrome's local extension storage (chrome.storage.local) to save:

  • Values you enter for a product, keyed by its ASIN: unit cost, shipping, other costs, sales share, and any fees, dimensions or category you corrected by hand, plus the date you last edited them.
  • Your settings: light or dark theme, FBA or FBM mode, default inbound shipping rate, ROI presets, FBM sales adjustment, and the "Stay open while browsing" preference.
  • Panel state for the current browsing session, so the panel can stay open as you move between products.

This data stays on your computer. It is not synced to your Google account, and it is not sent to EcomsTool or any third party. The extension does not keep a history of pages you visit; it only stores values you typed yourself.

What the extension does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, telemetry, tracking pixels, or cookies
  • No advertising
  • No remote servers and no remotely hosted code. All code ships inside the extension package.
  • No access to your Amazon account, Seller Central, orders, purchase history, or payment details
  • No access to websites other than amazon.com product pages

Clipboard

When you click Copy row, the extension writes a line of calculated values to your clipboard so you can paste it into a spreadsheet. It only writes when you click, and it never reads your clipboard.

Sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent, transfer, or share any user data with anyone. EcomsTool's use of information received through the extension complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Deleting your data

Use Settings → Clear saved data in the panel to erase everything the extension has stored. Removing the extension from Chrome also deletes all of its stored data.

Children

The extension is a business tool for sellers and is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If the extension ever starts handling data differently, we will update this page and its "Last updated" date before releasing that version, and note the change in the release notes.
