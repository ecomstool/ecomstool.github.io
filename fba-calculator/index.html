Amazon FBA & FBM Calculator: Profit, ROI & Monthly Sales

Open any amazon.com product page and see what you'd actually make: fees that match Amazon's own revenue calculator, profit, ROI and estimated monthly sales.

Add to Chrome — soon Privacy policy

What it does

  • Fees that match Amazon. Uses the current US rate card, including price bands, holiday peak rates, the fuel and logistics surcharge, and inbound placement fees.
  • FBA and FBM side by side. One click to switch. FBM handles free-shipping and paid-shipping listings correctly.
  • Referral fee by category. 8%, 12%, 15% and tiered categories, picked automatically and easy to correct.
  • Monthly sales and profit. Estimated from Amazon's "bought in past month" label and the product's Best Sellers Rank.
  • ROI presets. Tap a target ROI and the price updates to the lowest price that hits it.
  • Remembers your costs. Costs you enter for a product come back next time you open it.
  • Copy to your sheet. One click copies a tab-separated row that pastes straight into Google Sheets or Excel.

How to use it

Open an amazon.com product page, then click the extension icon in your toolbar or press Alt+Shift+F. The panel opens on the right. Enter your unit cost and the rest is calculated for you.

Private by design

The calculator reads the product details shown on the page you're viewing, does the math in your browser, and saves your inputs on your device. Nothing is sent to EcomsTool or anyone else. Read the privacy policy.

Estimates, not guarantees. Fees and sales figures are estimates. Always confirm final numbers in Seller Central before you buy inventory. Currently supports amazon.com (US) only.
